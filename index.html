Engineering review notes

Formulas: efficiency (%) = net generation / thermal input × 100; heat rate (kJ/kWh) = thermal input / net generation × 3,600; capacity factor (%) = net generation / (net capacity × hours) × 100. Values above 100% are flagged, never silently clamped. Startup/shutdown, ambient conditions, fuel quality, and auxiliary-load adjustments are not modelled.

Verification checklist

Before a reported value is promoted to a validated record

1
Open the original publication

Confirm title, publisher, publication date and exact wording.

2
Confirm metric, units and period

Do not infer a reporting interval that the source does not state.

3
Confirm aggregation scope

Keep single-unit, single-plant and multi-plant totals distinct.

4
Record evidence and limitations

Maintain source URL, retrieval date, confidence and reviewer notes.
